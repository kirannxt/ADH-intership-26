{% if assessment_type == 'adhd' %}

About ADHD Screening

This screen is inspired by the ASRS (Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale) developed by the WHO. A high score indicates traits that may warrant professional evaluation — it does not confirm ADHD. Many conditions share these traits, and only a clinician can make a diagnosis through a full assessment.

{% elif assessment_type == 'anxiety' %}

About Anxiety Screening

This screen is inspired by the GAD-7 (Generalised Anxiety Disorder 7-item scale). Scores of 8–15 suggest moderate anxiety; scores ≥16 suggest severe anxiety. Anxiety symptoms are common and treatable — speaking with a GP is a good first step.

{% elif assessment_type == 'depression' %}

About Depression Screening

This screen is inspired by the PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9). Depression is a medical condition — not a personal failing. If your score is 7 or above, please consider speaking with your GP. Effective treatments are available.

{% elif assessment_type == 'stress' %}

About Perceived Stress

Based on Cohen's PSS-10, this measures how stressed you feel — not an objective measure of stressors in your life. High perceived stress is associated with health impacts over time. Stress management strategies and professional support can both help.

{% elif assessment_type == 'learning' %}

About Learning Styles

Based on the VARK model (Fleming, 1992). Learning style preferences are tendencies, not rigid categories — most people use multiple styles. This result can help you choose study strategies that feel more natural. It is not a measure of intelligence or ability.

{% elif assessment_type == 'eq' %}

About Emotional Intelligence

Based on established EI frameworks (Mayer–Salovey–Caruso, Goleman). Emotional intelligence is a set of learnable skills — not a fixed trait. Lower scores indicate areas with potential for development, not personal deficiencies.

{% endif %}
What this result does not mean ▾
  • It is not a clinical diagnosis of any condition.
  • It does not define your character, intelligence, or worth.
  • It is not a permanent measure — it reflects today's responses.
  • It should not be used to make medical or major life decisions.
  • It is not a substitute for professional evaluation.
