🩺 When to Seek Professional Support

Automated assessments have significant limitations. Consider speaking with a qualified professional if any of the following apply:

  • You are experiencing persistent low mood, anxiety, or difficulty coping.
  • Your results surprised you or raised concerns you did not expect.
  • You want to understand your results in a personal context.
  • You are making an important life or career decision and want professional input.
  • You are supporting someone else who may need help.
